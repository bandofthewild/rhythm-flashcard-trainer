Rhythm Flashcard Trainer

Fun, Practical Rhythm Drilling · Individual or Ensemble Sight‑Reading

Project rhythm flash cards for the whole ensemble to read and count together. Choose a meter, difficulty level, and groove; press Play for a count‑off and a metered pulse that sweeps the subdivisions in time. Cards range from a single measure to multi‑bar phrases across simple, compound, cut, and asymmetrical meters — a new card each turn, or hold one to work it.

Rhythm Flashcard Trainer — Quick Tour

Get Going

  • Pick a Time Signature and a Difficulty Level, then press Play.
  • You get a Get Ready count‑off, then Count It — the pulse sweeps the subdivisions while the class counts in "1 e & a."
  • Tap Combine to mix levels/complexities; use Rhythm Customization to pick exact note values.

Run the Drill

  • Cards: Auto flips each turn; Hold stays put — use Next ▶ / ◀ (or ← →) to advance on your cue.
  • Tap the card or press R to Reveal the counting; press F for Full‑Screen.
  • Steps adds a Clap It pass; Advanced Options hold card length, syllable reveal, "Hear It," and count‑off length.

Use Your Own Music

  • Set Groove to Off for a silent count‑along over your own recording or ensemble.
  • Tap the beat (or press the spacebar) to match your track's tempo.
  • While running, tap a four‑count to re‑sync on the next downbeat. Save Current or Copy Link to keep a setup.

Show the "1 e & a" syllables under the notes. Reveal last bar keeps them hidden while students read, then reveals the counting to check — near the end of the card, or across the whole card during the Clap It pass.
