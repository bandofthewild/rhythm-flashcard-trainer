Rhythm Flashcard Trainer

A fun, practical way to drill rhythms — procedurally generated at all difficulty levels and in all types of meter.

  • ✓Flashcard Rhythm Reading: A new rhythm each turn — or Hold a card to drill it — with the counting revealed below on your cue.
  • ✓Counting Beats to the Beat: Press Play for a count-off, then count the rhythm, then clap or play it. Choose from a selection of provided grooves as a backbeat, or use the tempo-tap feature to pull in your own music to practice rhythms with.
  • ✓Every Meter, 6 Difficulty Levels: Simple, compound, cut time & asymmetrical meters — from whole notes to 16th-note triplets and beyond.
  • ✓Made for Individuals or the Full Classroom: A great practice routine for students at home, while full-screen mode on the projector makes it a perfect fit for full-ensemble drilling.
